Support

Need help with a Funnermentals game, found a bug, or have an idea? Email support@funnermentals.com. A real person (the developer) reads every message and replies within two business days.

  • Email support

Reporting a bug

To help me fix it quickly, please include:

  • The game and its version (shown in the game's settings)
  • Your device (for example, iPhone 16 or MacBook Air) and its iOS, iPadOS or macOS version
  • What you did, what you expected, and what happened instead
  • A screenshot or screen recording, if you can

Frequently asked questions

How do I remove ads?

Open the game's settings and choose Remove Ads. It's a one-time purchase that removes ads permanently on every device signed in to the same Apple Account.

I bought Remove Ads but I'm seeing ads on a new device.

Open the game's settings and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy it.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple, so refunds are too. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com to request one.

I saw an inappropriate ad.

Sorry about that. Email a screenshot or description to support@funnermentals.com and I'll block it.

What data does the game collect?

Very little. See the Privacy Policy for details, including how to ask for your data to be deleted.
